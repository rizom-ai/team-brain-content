Rizom's organizational structure is inspired by the rhizome, a plant stem that grows sideways underground while connecting to vast fungal networks. This metaphor translates into an organic, decentralized, and aligned-interest-based organizational model. The ecosystem operates through four key mechanisms: sharing expertise and resources across the professional network, regenerating collective value through fair compensation models, growing sustainably via curated connections and meaningful collaborations, and adapting together through open communication and continuous learning. This resilient structure allows the organization to survive damage, support growth from any point, and quickly adapt to changing conditions. The rhizome model emphasizes interconnectedness and mutual support rather than hierarchical control.
